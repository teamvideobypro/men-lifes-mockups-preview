Phase 1 • Internal & Presentation Groundwork

Weeks 1 to 4: Build the man

Weeks 1–4 • Sessions 1–5

Week 1 · Module 1: Onboarding

Everything starts with you. You can't have the healthy relationship you want with someone else until you have a healthy relationship with yourself. You take an honest inventory with your Client Profile and two assessments, learn how dating works from the first swipe to a relationship, and name exactly what you want.

Lessons
Your Client Profile
Your SMV Assessment
Your Dating Conduct Scenario Questionnaire
Orientation and the dating process
Dating goals and standards
Your sessions with Bill (1 and 2): Session 1, early in the week: your story, where you are and what you want, how the 12 weeks work, and how to complete every assessment and form. Session 2, at the end of the week: your results and your Client Profile, assets first, your top opportunities, and your signed Commitment and Accountability Agreement.
What you build:your Client Profile, both assessments, your dating goal and your standards.

Week 2 · Module 2: Evaluation & Gameplan

Read your results like a coach, not a judge. You find the one bottleneck holding you back and build a plan for your presentation and habits. Then you sharpen the whole man: responsibility, lifestyle, grooming, clothing and the energy you bring into a room.

Lessons
Understanding your Client Profile
Understanding your SMV Assessment
Understanding your Dating Conduct Scenario Assessment
Where we stand: opportunities for improvement
Building your personal game plan
Personal responsibility and lifestyle
Grooming and clothing
Energy and physical presence
Your session with Bill (3): We read your Client Profile and assessments together, review your grooming, clothing fit, energy and photo readiness, pick three changes, lock in your game plan and start your photo brief.
What you build:your bottleneck, a 30-day presentation plan and a personal game plan with three priorities, dates, a budget and a weekly review.

Week 3 · Module 3: Photo Strategy & Photo Production

Photos are the gatekeeper. They're the first thing she sees, and they usually decide whether she reads anything else. You audit your current photos, plan the story you want them to tell, and shoot a new set the right way: setup, prep, light and the six shots every profile needs.

Lessons
Auditing your current photos
Planning the visual story
Your setup, your prep and your light
Shots 1 to 3: hero, full body, lifestyle
Shots 4 to 6, editing and testing
Selecting and sequencing
Your session with Bill (4): We review your current set, complete your shot list, and choose from your new images: what each photo adds, and which repeats or misleading edits to cut.
What you build:your current photo lineup, a shoot brief and a new set in the right order.

Week 4 · Module 4: Building Your Online Profile

See your profile the way she sees it. Then write the words that turn a glance at your photos into a reason to start a conversation: a bio and prompts with real specifics, an honest statement of what you want, and your real age, owned with pride.

Lessons
What a woman sees
Messages that get ignored, and messages that get answered
Hobbies and skills: your secret advantage
Bios, prompts and stated intentions
Your real age, owned with pride
Your session with Bill (5): We walk through a woman's feed and inbox, turn vague statements into real details, and compare two of your bio drafts.
What you build:her side of the screen, your bio and your prompts.
Phase 2 • Going Live & Strategic Execution

Weeks 5 to 8: Launch, lock in your mindset, and choose wisely

Weeks 5–8 • Sessions 6–9

Week 5 · Module 5: Launching Your Profile

Everything you built in Weeks 2 to 4 goes live. You assemble and launch a complete profile, run a privacy check, and set up a simple way to test one change at a time, so you learn what works instead of guessing.

Lessons
Launch day: one man, one story
Test, don't guess
Your session with Bill (6): We assemble your final profile, run the privacy check, and set up your change log and your first review date.
What you build:a complete, live profile and a change log.

Week 6 · Module 6: Emotional & Mental Preparation

Your profile is live, and how you handle attention, silence and rejection shapes everything that follows. You build confidence grounded in what you do, set boundaries on your time, money and self-respect, and hold your frame even when the attraction is strong. You also get a straight look at the red, blue, purple and black pill ideas, and the Men's Life Mastery way. At the end of the week, you retake both assessments for your midpoint review.

Lessons
Confidence is something you do
Emotional fitness: feel it, don't text it
Boundaries: decisions about you, not rules for her
Simping: what it is, and what it costs you
Frame: living your values when it counts
Red pill, blue pill, purple pill or black pill
What each pill gets right, and the Men's Life Mastery way
Your session with Bill (7): We role-play the moments that test you, like an unanswered message, a request for an expensive outing and a push for private information, and practice a calm response, a fair alternative or an exit.
What you build:your boundary responses and a discipline routine.

Week 7 · Module 7: Understanding Dating Apps

The second half starts with your midpoint review: how far you've come, measured honestly. Then you see how attention really works on the apps, why most men stay invisible while a few stand out, and what the competition looks like. You choose one or two platforms that fit your goal and set a time and money budget before you pay for any features.

Lessons
Your midpoint review, measured honestly
The numbers, and why most men stay invisible
Why some men stand out
What other men look like
Choosing platforms and using them well
Budget and paid features
Your session with Bill (8): We compare your midpoint assessments with intake, look at how six other men present themselves, find your own gaps, and set your platform choice, budget and commitments.
What you build:your midpoint review, your platform choice and your budget.

Week 8 · Module 8: Vetting Women

Don't accept her just because she accepts you. Dating is a two-way interview, and you're choosing too. You learn to talk naturally about intentions and lifestyle, watch for honesty, respect and follow-through, verify the basics for a safe meet, and decide whether to proceed, pause or exit. You also cover green flags, red flags, the flags she's reading in you, and the honest truth about hypergamy.

Lessons
Dating is a two-way interview
Intentions and practical compatibility
Observations and patterns
Reasonable verification and safety
Proceed, pause or exit
Green flags
Red flags: degrees, dealbreakers and when to walk
The mirror: she's reading your flags too
Hypergamy: what it is, and what women weigh
Clear the bar, don't chase the ladder
Your session with Bill (9): We work through three real-world cases (strong chemistry with mismatched intentions, missing information, and consistent mutual effort) and practice a natural follow-up question for each.
What you build:a compatibility worksheet and a clear next decision for every match.
Phase 3 • Conversation Mastery & In-Person Connection

Weeks 9 to 12: From first message to first meet, and beyond

Weeks 9–12 • Sessions 10–13

Week 9 · Module 9: Openers & Mutual Conversation

The first of three modules that take you from the first message to the first meet. You write openers that fit her profile, balance sharing with asking, bring warmth and humor, and read whether she's really participating.

Lessons
Finding a useful opening
Sharing and asking
Warmth, humor and interest
Reading participation
Your session with Bill (10): We write five personalized openers from practice profiles, rewrite an interview-style exchange, and practice keeping a warm conversation going without a script.
What you build:personalized openers and conversation practice.

Week 10 · Module 10: Messaging & Text Game, Calls, & Videos

This is where most promising matches stall. You learn to text with a purpose, keep the pace mutual, follow up without chasing, respond calmly to hesitation, know when to stop, and use a short call or video call to move things toward a meet.

Lessons
Texting with a purpose
Pacing and reciprocity
Following up, double texting, and letting go
Responding to hesitation
Knowing when to stop
A short call or video call, when useful
Your session with Bill (11): We practice your replies to a busy week, a request to stay on the app, an expensive-date suggestion, a cancellation and a clear no, then rehearse a short call.
What you build:your texting decisions, response practice and a call outline.

Week 11 · Module 11: First Meets, Dates, & Follow Throughs

Online interest becomes an in-person meeting. You make a clear invitation, plan a public, affordable first meet, handle confirming and rescheduling, and show up prepared. You learn the magic of listening, how to handle confidence tests and touchy subjects with class, how to talk about your ex, how to end the meeting with respect, and how to follow up honestly. And we cover the elephant in the room: sex, consent, and knowing your app.

Lessons
A clear invitation
The meet and greet, and who pays
Less is more: the magic of listening
Confirming, rescheduling and timing
Preparing, arriving and knowing your audience
Chivalry today
Conversation and confidence tests
Talking about your ex
Touchy subjects: handle them with class
Ending the meeting, with respect
The elephant in the room: the honest truth, the playbook and knowing your app
Reflection and follow-up
Your session with Bill (12): We rehearse a coffee invitation and a reschedule, choose two local public spots, and practice the conversation from hello to goodbye, including a quiet moment, an uncomfortable topic, a second-date invitation and a kind closing message.
What you build:a first-meet plan, a date reflection and follow-up drafts.

Week 12 · Module 12: Review & 90 Day Plan

The finish line. You retake both assessments and measure how far you've come with the same yardstick you started with. You learn to judge your decisions and not just your results, get ready for the conversation about where a relationship is going, and build a 90-day plan that keeps the man growing after coaching ends.

Lessons
How far you've come, measured honestly
Judge your decisions, not just your results
When it gets real: talking about where it's going
Keep the man growing: your next 90 days
Your session with Bill (13): We compare where you started with where you are now, rehearse a relationship alignment conversation, choose your next three priorities and complete your 90-day schedule.
What you build:your final review and a 90-day maintenance plan.
