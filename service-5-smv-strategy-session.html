THE 7 EVALUATION AREAS

What the SMV Assessment measures

Women don't just notice your face. Over the first few dates, they notice the whole man: how you look and live, how you handle money and people, and how you show up when things get real. The assessment gives you 150 real situations, and asks for the best response to each, across seven areas:

01

Physical Value

Your overall visual presentation: style, grooming, fitness, posture, somatic presence, and how immediately you communicate vitality and masculine strength.

02

Status and Resources

Your career trajectory, competence, ambition, financial responsibility, and whether you convey stability and forward momentum in life.

03

Social Value

Your social circle, dynamic network, respect among high-caliber men, social proof, and ease when navigating varied social environments.

04

Masculine Polarity

Your decisiveness, emotional groundedness, ability to hold frame under pressure, quiet confidence, and natural leadership.

05

Emotional and Relationship Value

Emotional fitness, integrity, maturity, healthy boundaries, consistency, and how you navigate tension or conflict without reactivity.

06

Lifestyle and Identity

Your genuine passions, active routine, independent interests, personal standards, and living a life that naturally invites others in.

07

Sexual and Romantic Value

Flirting, conversational chemistry, calibration, romantic tension, eye contact, and clear, respectful romantic intent.

It measures your judgment, not your worth, your looks or how every woman will see you. It's a coaching tool that shows you where to work.
